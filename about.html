About Portable Slot Grill

Who publishes Portable Slot Grill, what it covers, and where product facts come from.

Portable Slot Grill is a set of practical guides for charcoal grilling away from home and for small spaces at home.

Who publishes it

Slot Grill. The Slot Grill is a collapsible carbon steel charcoal grill made in the USA by Keith in Buna, Texas, in a Compact and a Full Size, with an optional griddle.

How we handle facts

  • Specs and prices come from SlotGrill.com, which is the authority.
  • Food temperatures follow USDA minimums.
  • No invented reviews, ratings or testimonials.
