Portable Grill Packing Checklist

A complete packing checklist for portable charcoal grilling: grill kit, fire kit, food kit and cleanup kit.

Grill kit

  • Slot Grill body, two grates and the two grate keys (they double as bottle openers)
  • Griddle plate(s), if you have the griddle add-on
  • Bag or box for the packed grill

Fire kit

  • Chimney starter
  • Charcoal (sealed bin or bag) or dry cooking wood where allowed
  • Lighter, newspaper or natural starters
  • Heat-safe gloves

Cooking kit

  • Long tongs and a spatula; scraper
  • Instant-read thermometer
  • Separate trays for raw and cooked
  • Foil, oil, salt and pepper

Food kit

  • Cooler, plenty of ice, raw meat sealed at the bottom
  • Prepped food labeled by cook order
  • Water, soap and paper towels for hands

Cleanup kit

  • Lidded metal can for ash
  • Water or extinguisher
  • Trash bags
  • Oil or Seasoning Balm for the steel

Pack-up routine

  1. Let the coals burn down; never leave them unattended.
  2. When completely cold, ash into a lidded metal can or the site's bin.
  3. Scrape grates, wipe cooking surfaces with a thin coat of oil or Seasoning Balm.
  4. Lift grates, slide out the V side panel, pull the end pieces, stack.
  5. Bag it so soot stays off your gear.

Away-from-home ground rules

  • Ask first. Venue, park, campground, beach, RV park and building rules decide whether charcoal is allowed, and where.
  • Check for burn bans on dry, windy days.
  • Outdoors only. Never under an awning, in a tent, camper, garage, vehicle or boat cabin: charcoal makes carbon monoxide.
  • Non-combustible ground with clearance from vehicles, tents, dunes, brush and people.
  • Never unattended; ash goes in a metal can when cold.
