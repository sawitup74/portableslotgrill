Portable Grilling Glossary

Plain definitions of portable charcoal grilling terms: air gap, chimney starter, flat-pack, two-zone, griddle and more.

Air gap
On the Slot Grill, the space between the table and the bottom of the coal bed: 2⅞" Compact, 3⅝" Full Size.
Chimney starter
A metal tube for lighting charcoal with paper.
Flat-pack
A grill that comes apart into flat plates for carrying.
Two-zone fire
Coals on one side, none on the other.
Griddle (plancha)
A flat steel cooking plate.
Grate key
Slot Grill's grate-lifting tool; doubles as a bottle opener.
Carbon steel
Strong steel that takes seasoning and rusts if left bare and wet.
Seasoning
Oil baked onto steel as a protective coating.
Lump charcoal
Charred hardwood; lights fast and hot.
Briquettes
Pressed charcoal; steady and long.
Leave No Trace
Outdoor ethic: pack out everything, leave the site as found.
Tab and slot
How the Slot Grill's panels lock together by hand.
