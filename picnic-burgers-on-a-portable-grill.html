Picnic Burgers on a Portable Grill

Classic burgers prepped at home and grilled at the park or tailgate on a portable charcoal grill.

Form the patties at home, stack them between parchment in a sealed container, and the cook at the park takes 15 minutes.

Serves 4Time 15 minutes at the grillGrill Slot Grill Compact

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb ground beef, 80/20, formed into 4 patties with a thumbprint in the center
  • Salt and pepper
  • 4 buns, cheese, lettuce, tomato, onion

Method

  1. At home: form patties, stack with parchment, seal, keep cold.
  2. Light a chimney; build a two-zone fire.
  3. Season patties; grill over coals 3 to 4 minutes per side.
  4. Add cheese; move to the cool side if flaring.
  5. Cook to 160°F. Toast buns on the cool side.

Tips

  • Press only once with the thumbprint, not with the spatula during cooking.

Safe internal temperatures

Check the thickest part with an instant-read thermometer, away from bone. These are USDA minimum safe temperatures; tougher cuts like brisket and ribs are cooked well past them for tenderness.

FoodMinimum internal temperature
Beef, pork, lamb and veal steaks, chops and roasts145°F, then rest 3 minutes
Ground beef, pork, lamb and veal (burgers, fresh sausage)160°F
Chicken and turkey, whole, pieces or ground165°F
Fish and shellfish145°F, or flesh opaque and flakes with a fork
Egg dishes160°F; cook eggs until yolks and whites are firm
Precooked sausage, hot dogs and leftoversReheat to 165°F

Food safety without a kitchen

  • Cooler at 40°F or below; raw meat sealed at the bottom.
  • Separate tongs and trays for raw and cooked.
  • Perishables out no more than 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F).
  • Soap and water for hands; a thermometer for doneness.

Pack-up routine

  1. Let the coals burn down; never leave them unattended.
  2. When completely cold, ash into a lidded metal can or the site's bin.
  3. Scrape grates, wipe cooking surfaces with a thin coat of oil or Seasoning Balm.
  4. Lift grates, slide out the V side panel, pull the end pieces, stack.
  5. Bag it so soot stays off your gear.
