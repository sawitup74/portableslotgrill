Portable Grilling FAQ

Answers about portable charcoal grilling: where it's allowed, packing, safety, food and the Slot Grill.

All the questions from across the site, grouped by topic.

Where to Grill

Can I grill in a stadium parking lot?

Many venues allow it with rules, some don't. Check the venue's tailgating policy before you go. Read: Tailgate Grilling Guide: Portable Charcoal Grill in the Parking Lot

Can I set the grill on my tailgate?

No. Set it on the pavement with clearance from the vehicle. Never cook on a tailgate or in a truck bed. Read: Tailgate Grilling Guide: Portable Charcoal Grill in the Parking Lot

Can I cook inside my tent if it's raining?

Never. Charcoal produces carbon monoxide. Cook in the open and keep the fire away from the tent. Read: Campsite Grilling Guide

Where should I store a charcoal grill in an RV?

Cold, empty and bagged, in a basement bay or behind a seat, with charcoal in a sealed bin. Read: RV Grilling Guide

Can I bury hot coals in the sand?

No. Buried coals stay hot for hours. Drown and stir, then pack ash out or use designated bins. Read: Beach Grilling Guide

Can I put a charcoal grill on a picnic table?

Not on a wooden table. Set it on concrete, gravel or bare dirt. Read: Park and Picnic Area Grilling

Can I use a charcoal grill on an apartment balcony?

It depends on your building rules and local fire code; many restrict it. Check before buying or lighting. Read: Backyard and Small Patio Grilling

Can I grill on my boat?

Not with a charcoal grill. Carry it as cargo and cook on shore where fires are permitted. Read: Fishing and Boat Day Shore Cooking

Transport & Storage

How soon can I pack the grill after cooking?

Only when the steel and ash are completely cold. Read: Packing a Portable Grill in a Car Trunk

Is the Slot Grill light enough for backpacking?

It's heavy steel. The Compact (about 14 lb) is reasonable for paddling or a short walk-in, not ultralight backpacking. Read: Carrying a Portable Grill by Kayak, Canoe or on Foot

Will a carbon steel grill rust in storage?

It can if stored damp or bare. Oil the cooking surfaces and store it dry. Read: Storing a Portable Grill at Home

Do the griddle plates fit both Slot Grills?

Slot Grill sells griddle options for both the Compact and the Full Size, in one- or two-plate configurations. Read: Griddle Plates on the Go

On-the-Go How-Tos

Can I use lighter fluid?

You can, but a chimney or natural starters work better. Never add lighter fluid to lit coals, and never use gasoline. Read: Lighting Charcoal Away From Home

How long can food sit out at a picnic?

No more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F. Read: Cooler and Food Safety for Portable Grilling

How do I lower the heat on a grill with no vents?

Move food to the cool zone, spread the coals thinner, or let the fire burn down. Read: Controlling Heat on a Portable Grill

Does the air gap make it safe on a wood deck?

No. The air gap helps minimize radiant heat, but the surface must still be non-combustible. Read: Setting Up a Portable Grill on Different Surfaces

How long does charcoal ash stay hot?

Many hours. Treat it as hot until it's cold to the touch. Read: Ash Disposal and Leave No Trace
